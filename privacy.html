Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Fortune Cheatkey Uploader ("the Service"), operated by 은하 운세소 (Galaxy Oracle Studio), does with information it receives from TikTok, YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta).

1. YouTube API Services

The Service uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel. By using the Service to post to YouTube you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we receive

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, Google and Meta when you authorize the Service.
  • Basic account information: TikTok open ID, nickname, username and avatar; YouTube channel ID, title and handle; Instagram account ID and username, and the connected Facebook Page name.
  • Publishing results for the videos you post (post, video or media IDs, status and links).

The Service does not access your other videos, comments, messages, subscribers, analytics or any data about other users.

3. How we use it

Only to post the videos you choose to your own accounts, to show you which account or channel is connected, and to avoid posting the same video twice. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a file readable only by that user (for example ~/.config/youtube/token.json). They are never written into source code, shared folders or any server we run. Posting records contain only video IDs and dates, no tokens. The Service does not use cookies or tracking technologies; the only data it stores on your device is the token and posting-record files described here.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform you are posting to (TikTok, YouTube/Google or Instagram/Meta), as required to publish your videos.

6. Revoking access and deletion

  • YouTube / Google: remove the Service at Google security settings → Third-party apps & services.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram / Facebook: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations), and remove the Service.

Deleting the local token files removes all stored tokens. Stored account information and posting records are deleted on request, or within 30 days after access is revoked. To ask for deletion or with any question, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of this page shows the latest version.
