Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Fortune Cheatkey Uploader ("the Service") does with information from TikTok.

1. Information we receive

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok when you authorize the Service.
  • Basic account information (open ID, nickname, username, avatar) and the posting options TikTok returns for your account.
  • Publishing results for the videos you post (publish ID and status).

2. How we use it

Only to post the videos you choose to your own TikTok account, to show you which account is connected, and to avoid posting the same video twice. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer in a file readable only by that user. They are never written into source code or shared folders. Posting records contain no tokens.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, as required to publish your videos.

5. Deletion

You can revoke the Service's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored tokens. To ask for deletion or with any question, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).
