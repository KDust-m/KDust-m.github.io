Fortune Cheatkey Uploader

Fortune Cheatkey Uploader is a personal publishing tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @galaxy.oracle_studio. Every day it takes short vertical videos that the creator produced in advance (daily horoscope content: zodiac sign and Chinese zodiac rankings) and posts them to the creator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, using the caption and hashtags the creator wrote.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes.
  2. The tool shows the connected account's nickname and the privacy options TikTok allows for that account.
  3. The creator chooses the privacy level and the videos to post. The tool uploads the video files and captions to TikTok.
  4. The tool checks the publishing status and records which videos were posted so the same video is not posted twice.

The tool only posts to the account of the creator who authorized it. It does not read, collect or post to any other user's account.
